# Unit economics at 1, 10 and 50 customers (baseline: one OVHcloud VPS-2 per customer), 2026-10-28

Status: **model with explicit assumptions, not a forecast, not a quote, no recommendation beyond the stated baseline.**
Nothing was bought, contacted or run. Source access date for every cited page: **2026-10-28**. Related:
`docs/research/cloud-decision-sheet-2026-10-28.md` (OVH facts, documented vs assumed table), `docs/research/ai-costs-2026-10-28.md`
(R3), ADR-0059, ADR-0066.

**A single pilot customer does not prove scaled economics.** At 1 customer almost every cost is fixed or hand-made; at 10 the
manual work is still tolerable; at 50 the fixed costs are spread but support, abuse handling and provisioning become the
dominant risks. The unmerged stack and the J1/J2 privacy blockers (corrected in source on `claude/block19`, statically,
NOT RUN) are not closed and no number below assumes they are.

## 1. Inputs

Price of one customer: EUR 60 tax-inclusive (owner's reference, ADR-0059, not validated). Net of 22% VAT: 60 / 1.22 =
**49.18** (VAT 10.82). Cost inputs, tagged D = documented on an official page, A = assumed placeholder (NOT a quote):

| Symbol | Meaning | Value (EUR/month unless noted) | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | net revenue per customer | 49.18 | derived |
| V | VPS-2, 12-month upfront rate per month | 7.21 (annual 86.52 ex VAT; 105.55 incl.) | D (ovhcloud.com/it/vps) |
| B | off-instance backup storage per customer | 1.50 | A (the included OVH backup is one 24 h copy in the same datacentre, so an off-instance copy is assumed; OVH object-storage price NOT FOUND) |
| P | payment fees per customer on gross 60 | 1.57 = 1.5% x 60 + 0.25 + 0.7% x 60 | D (Stripe Italy: EEA standard card 1.5% + EUR 0.25; Billing 0.7%; stripe.com/en-it/pricing). Premium EEA card 2.8% + 0.25 and Tax 0.5% would give 2.65; disputes EUR 20 each |
| A_L, A_M | AI + voice, light / medium | 3.73 / 16.23 (USD read as EUR, 1:1) | D prices, A usage (R3 combo B) |
| G | gateway + broker + monitoring node | 7.21 up to 10 customers; 14.42 at 50 (two VPS-2) | A sizing, D price |
| W | domain + email | 3.00 | A |
| K | control-plane DB/backup | 2.00 | A |
| C1 | compliance one-off (legal review, DPIA, DPO screening), amortised over 12 months | 5,000 -> 416.67/month | A placeholder, not a quote |
| C2 | compliance recurring | 100 (200 at 50) | A |
| h_s | security-maintenance hours per month | 4 / 8 / 20 at 1 / 10 / 50 | A |
| h_c | support hours per customer per month | 1.0 | A |
| r | labour rate | 40 EUR/h | A (opportunity cost of the operator's time, not a salary) |
| S | mix | "mix" = 50% light, 50% medium customers (AI 9.98 EUR average) | A |

Formulas (N customers, a = AI cost per customer):

- Variable cost per customer: v = V + B + a + P. Light 7.21 + 1.50 + 3.73 + 1.57 = 14.01; medium 26.51; mix 20.26 (AI 9.98).
- Shared cash cost per month: F(N) = G(N) + W + K + C1/12 + C2(N).
- Labour: L(N) = (h_s(N) + h_c x N) x r.
- Cash result: Pi_cash = N x (R - v) - F(N). Fully loaded result: Pi_full = Pi_cash - L(N).
- Per customer: divide by N.

documented prices; the point of the table is the structure, not the numbers.

Break-even number of customers (mix, contribution per customer 28.92 before shared costs):
- cash view, compliance one-off 0: F = 7.21 + 3 + 2 + 100 = 112.21 -> about **3.9** customers;
- cash view, compliance one-off 5,000: F = 528.88 -> about **18.3** customers;
- fully loaded with 0.5 h support per customer (contribution 28.92 - 20 = 8.92) and 8 security hours: about **95** customers
  (more than the 50 modelled; at that size the shared node count also rises, so this is a lower bound).

## 4. Annual-commitment cash flow per VM

Each VM is paid **12 months up front**: EUR 86.52 ex VAT (105.55 incl.) per customer, due before or at activation, renewed
automatically for the same period, not refundable and not cancellable early without paying the remainder (OVH Italian GTC art.
11.4 and 12.4; VPS terms art. 7-8; commitments doc). Total outlay: 1 VM 86.52 (105.55); 10 VMs 865.20 (1,055.54); 50 VMs 4,326
(5,277.20). The customer pays monthly, so the operator finances up to 11 months of VM cost per customer. A customer who leaves
after m months strands (12 - m) x 7.21: leaving after 2 months strands 72.10 (87.96 incl. VAT) per VM, against two months of
net revenue of 98.36 (before variable costs). Pre-provisioned spare VMs for fast onboarding carry
the same lock-in with no revenue. A pooled or monthly alternative was not priced here (monthly OVH price NOT FOUND).
The commitment also interacts with the "stop idle VM" saving considered in the old survey: stopping gives no refund, because
disabled-but-not-deleted VPS keep being billed (VPS terms art. 8).

## 5. Migration limits, 1 to 10 to 50

| Dimension | 1 customer | 10 customers | 50 customers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provisioning | hand-made through the Control Panel, acceptable | scripts needed; order API (cart/checkout) exists but exact EU endpoints and rate limits are not verified; each order is an annual payment decision | automation, inventory, reinstall and image pipeline are mandatory; failed or duplicate orders cost real money (non-refundable annual price) |
| Annual lock-in | 86.52 | 865.20; churn strands months already paid | 4,326; churn and spare capacity are a working-capital and margin risk |
| IP and abuse | one IP, reputation unknown | each customer's browser traffic and logins share the OVH range; spam/intrusion/bot clauses and SMTP-port blocking apply per service | abuse complaints, CAPTCHA/blocks by target sites and suspension risk (GTC art. 3.6, 7.3) become routine; OVH may cut a VPS with a compromise; per-VM egress policy and logs needed |
| Support | founder handles everything | tickets, takeover sessions via noVNC, 2FA help | needs a rota, runbooks, status page; support hours dominate cost (table above) |
| Backup and restore | included 24 h backup, same datacentre, restore minutes-hours; off-instance copy assumed | restore tests per customer needed; backup storage grows 75 GB x N | storage and restore time scale with N; OVH disclaims backup as a guarantee |
| Control plane | one node | gateway, broker, ledger reconciliation under load | capacity, HA, secrets rotation, budget-ledger reconciliation with vendor invoices |
| Compliance | pilot DPIA | processor register, sub-processor notices | DPO decision, DSAR process, incident process |
| Capacity | one VPS-2 | 8 GB with Chromium plus agent is unmeasured | noisy-neighbour variance across 50 shared hosts unmeasured |

## 6. Cost lines still missing from this model

Monitoring/log SaaS if not self-hosted, status page, error budget credits, secrets vault, customer data export on exit, refunds
and chargebacks beyond the EUR 20 dispute fee, accounting, insurance, key-person time for incident response, translation of legal
texts, taxes on profit, FX on USD vendors, growth marketing. None has a source.

## 7. What is NOT FOUND

Monthly OVH price and renewal price; Italy datacentre for VPS; EUR snapshot and premium backup prices; OVH object-storage
price; fee structure of any alternative payment provider (only Stripe Italy was read); any quote for legal/DPIA/DPO work;
measured usage; measured support load; AUP position on logged-in automation at scale.

No recommendation is made here beyond the stated baseline; the table shows that the baseline's economics depend on usage
mix, support load, compliance cost and the annual-commitment lock-in, none of which a single pilot can settle.
